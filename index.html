Twelve editions, one for each month.

Wander the archive and open whichever letter catches your eye.

Every card traces the Sun's journey through the sky over the course of the year.

Photograph the Sun at the same clock time all year and it draws a lopsided figure eight, the analemma. Earth's tilted axis swings it north and south; its elliptical orbit speeds and slows the Sun's apparent drift, stretching it east and west.
